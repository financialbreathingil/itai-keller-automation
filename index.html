We set up and run customer messaging automation for small businesses.

Our clients are course providers, schools and small service businesses in Israel. We connect to the accounts they already own, their Facebook Page, their Instagram account and their WhatsApp Business number, and make sure that nobody who asks them a question is left without an answer.

What the service does

Small businesses lose enquiries simply because messages arrive faster than one person can answer them. We build and operate the system that catches those enquiries and puts them in front of a human being.

Answers arriving enquiries

When someone comments on the client's post or messages their Page asking about courses, prices or registration, the system sends a short reply with the client's own contact details.

Alerts the client's staff

The person on the client's team gets a WhatsApp alert with what was asked and a link to the conversation, so a real person can follow up.

Keeps the client's contact list current

Every enquiry is written into the client's own contact list, so their staff can see who is still waiting for an answer.

Lead forms and website forms

Enquiries from the client's Facebook lead forms and their own website reach the same list, instead of sitting in separate inboxes.

How it works

  1. The client grants access to their own accounts. Each business connects its own Facebook Page, Instagram account and WhatsApp Business number, and can revoke that access at any time. The owner does this themselves through Meta's own permission dialog: connect a Facebook Page or connect a WhatsApp Business number.
  2. We configure the responses with the client. The wording of every automatic reply is written and approved by the client before it goes live.
  3. The system runs day to day. It replies, alerts the client's staff, and records each enquiry in that client's own contact list.
  4. A person takes it from there. The automation opens the door. The conversation itself is always handled by the client's own team.

What we do with the data

We use the information we receive from Meta only to provide this service to the client it belongs to: to reply to the person who wrote in, to alert that client's staff, and to keep that client's own contact list up to date. Each client's data is kept separate and is never mixed with another client's. We do not sell data and we do not use it for advertising to third parties. Full detail is in our privacy policy.
