Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 September 2026

This policy explains what information the messaging automation service operated by Itai Keller ("we") receives, how it is used, and how it can be deleted. The service is operated on behalf of business clients, on accounts those clients own.

Our role

Each business client owns its own Facebook Page, Instagram account, WhatsApp Business number and contact records. We act on that client's instructions, as their service provider. The client decides what the automatic replies say, who on their team receives alerts, and when access ends.

What information we receive

  • The content of a public comment or a message sent to the client's Page or Instagram account, and the name and profile identifier of the person who sent it.
  • Details submitted by a person through the client's lead form or the client's own website form, such as name, phone number and email address, where the person provided them.
  • Messages exchanged with the client's WhatsApp Business number.
  • Statistics about the client's own advertising and Page activity, used to report to that client.

We do not ask for and do not collect payment card details, government identity numbers, health information or any other special category of personal data.

How the information is used

  • To send the person who wrote in a reply on behalf of the client, containing the client's contact details.
  • To alert the client's own staff so that a person can answer.
  • To record the enquiry in that client's contact list, so the client can see who is waiting for an answer.
  • To produce reports for that client about their own enquiries and advertising.

We do not sell personal information, we do not share it with third parties for their own purposes, and we do not use one client's information for another client or for any purpose the client has not asked for.

Where the information is kept

Information is stored on services we use to operate the system, in accounts under our control or under the client's control, protected by access credentials limited to the people who operate the service. Access to a client's data is limited to that client's staff and to us.

How long it is kept

Enquiry records are kept in the client's contact list for as long as the client uses the service, because the client relies on them to follow up with people. When a client ends the service, their data is deleted within 30 days of their request, other than what we must keep by law.

Deleting your information

If you wrote to one of our clients and want your details deleted, send an email to financialbreathingil@gmail.com with the phone number or the name you used. We will delete the record from that client's contact list and confirm by email, normally within 7 days and in any case within 30 days.

If you are a business client, you can remove our access at any time from your own Facebook Business settings, and ask us in writing to delete the data we hold for you. This is done within 30 days.

Your rights

You may ask what information we hold about you, ask for it to be corrected, or ask for it to be deleted, by writing to the address above. If the information belongs to one of our clients' contact lists, we will handle the request together with that client.
